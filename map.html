Commune Map

Every municipal indicator this pipeline holds, drawn on the 565 Belgian communes. Pick an indicator; hover a commune for its value; click it to open its own profile page. Communes are shaded by rank, so each colour band holds roughly the same number of communes — that shows where a commune sits relative to the rest, which is what a national map can honestly show. Communes the source has no figure for are drawn flat, never as zero. See communes.html for the same figures as a table, all_data.html for national-level data, or local.html for a single commune's profile.

Boundaries: Statbel (Direction générale Statistique — Statistics Belgium), statistical sectors of 1 January 2026, reused under Statbel's Licence open data of 22 October 2015, the same document already recorded in docs/licences/. Changes were made: the 20,781 statistical sectors were merged into the 565 communes, the outlines simplified to a 50 m tolerance so the file is small enough to send to a browser, and the coordinates converted from Belgian Lambert 2008 (EPSG:3812) to longitude/latitude. The source is accurate to 1:10,000; these simplified outlines are not, and are drawn for comparison, never for measurement or for locating a boundary on the ground.

Source: ONEM/RVA (Office National de l'Emploi — Rijksdienst voor Arbeidsvoorziening), for the unemployment, temporary-unemployment, part-time-benefit and activation columns. Date of the information used: the files fetched for this build, covering 2017 onwards; the current year is a part-year average and is marked provisional, and each row carries its own status letter. Cells ONEM withholds for privacy (fewer than 10 people) are shown as suppressed, never as zero.

Source: Police Fédérale / Federale Politie (Direction de l'information policière et des moyens ICT), for the house-burglary, car-theft, vehicle-theft and domestic-violence rate columns. Reused under the federal police's own condition, quoted in full in docs/data_catalog.md: correctly credit the source as stated here. That condition says nothing about permitted uses beyond attribution — not CC BY 4.0, which does not apply to this source, and not a stated grant of commercial reuse either, unlike Statbel and ONEM above. Every one of these rates is resolved onto the commune boundaries in force through 2024, not the current map, for every year shown — including years long before that map existed, since that is how the source itself publishes its own history. The most recent year of each series is marked provisional; its exact reporting window is not stated by the source.
Reads data/geo/communes.geojson and public/data/indicators/{id}.json from GitHub Pages — no server, no map tiles, no third-party requests. The indicator list is read from public/data/metadata/indicators.json, so a new municipal dataset appears in the picker on its own with no change to this page.
